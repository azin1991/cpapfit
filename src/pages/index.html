About Us

CPAP Fit is a landmark CPAP clinic dedicated to educating and serving patients who require CPAP therapy. As an ADP-registered vendor, we carry a wide variety of respiratory products from reputable CPAP manufacturers. We have a team of CPAP educators that are registered sleep technologists (RPSGT) and respiratory therapists (RT) available to our patients. Our CPAP educators have the extensive sleep knowledge and experience needed to help you manage your CPAP therapy.

Feel free to contact us either by phone, email, or visit our CPAP clinic during our business hours to speak with one of our qualified CPAP educators.

Our commitment

We are committed to providing only the highest quality CPAP equipment and supplies to our patients. We only provide products from reputable manufacturers and suppliers and will honour all warranties extended by them.

02

Comprehensive Education

As a CPAP clinic, education is central to what we do. Our team of CPAP educators understand that having obstructive sleep apnea is difficult and finding the right treatment can be challenging. If you have questions about your current CPAP therapy (even if you purchased CPAP with another provider), our CPAP educators can offer you comprehensive education to succeed with your therapy.

Professional Services

Provided by our registered sleep technologists (RPSGT) and respiratory therapists (RT).

  • Rental Trials

    Try CPAP therapy at home before you buy any equipment.

  • CPAP therapy education

    Learn how to use and care for your machine and mask.

  • Mask fitting

    Find a mask that fits comfortably and seals well.

  • Mask adjustments and troubleshooting

    Help with leaks, discomfort and fit problems.

  • Pressure changes

    Only with a current prescription.

  • Pressure check

    Confirm your machine delivers the prescribed pressure.

  • Filter and tubing check

    Inspect filters and tubing and replace them when needed.
