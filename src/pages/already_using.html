Already Using CPAP?

Whether you are new or a long time user of CPAP, CPAP FIT will help you get the most out of your CPAP therapy. Our educators offer comprehensive consultations to show you how to properly use and care for your CPAP mask and machine. Many patients are not aware of all the features that their machines have. Let our educators show you how to access your compliance data so you can better track your sleep!

  • Use & care for your mask and machine
  • Discover your machine's features
  • Access your compliance data

Struggling with your CPAP therapy?

It is extremely difficult to succeed with CPAP therapy without comprehensive education from a qualified CPAP educator. Many people who have tried CPAP therapy and failed never received the proper instruction needed to succeed. Let our educators get you back on track! Bring your CPAP machine and mask to us and our educators will give you meaningful instruction to get back onto your CPAP therapy.
