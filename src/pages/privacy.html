Privacy

How this website handles your information

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers of its own, and it has no accounts. Like most websites, our web host keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages you visited, to keep the site secure and working.

Maps and videos

Some pages show content from other companies, which may set their own cookies and collect information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps on the Home and Contact pages (Google Privacy Policy).
  • YouTube videos on the Rental Trials page. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which doesn't store viewing information until you play a video (Google Privacy Policy).

Contact form, email and phone

When you send our contact form, we receive your name, email, phone number (if given), preferred location and message by email. If you use the form, email or call us, we use your contact details only to answer you and to arrange your care. Please don't send health card numbers or medical details through the form or by email; we'll discuss your care by phone or in person.
